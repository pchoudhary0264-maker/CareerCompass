📊 Chartered Accountant

About

Chartered Accountants manage financial records, taxation, auditing, and business planning.

📚 Required Subjects

Commerce is preferred, but students from other streams can also pursue CA.

🧠 Skills Required

Accounting, Mathematics, Analysis, Attention to Detail.

🎓 Education

CA Foundation, Intermediate, and Final examinations conducted by ICAI.

💼 Career Opportunities

Auditor, Tax Consultant, Financial Advisor, Finance Manager.

💰 Average Salary

₹7–25 LPA

📈 Future Scope

Excellent opportunities in multinational companies, banks, and private practice.
