🎨 Graphic Designer

About

Graphic Designers create logos, posters, social media graphics, advertisements, and digital artwork.

📚 Required Subjects

Any stream with interest in Art and Design.

🧠 Skills Required

Creativity, Design, Communication, Adobe Photoshop, Canva, Figma.

🎓 Education

B.Des., Diploma in Graphic Design, or related courses.

💼 Career Opportunities

UI/UX Designer, Brand Designer, Illustrator, Motion Graphic Designer.

💰 Average Salary

₹3–10 LPA

📈 Future Scope

Growing demand in advertising, gaming, web design, and digital marketing.
