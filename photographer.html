📸 Photographer

About

Photographers capture images for events, fashion, wildlife, sports, travel, journalism, and commercial purposes using creativity and technical skills.

📚 Required Subjects

Any stream. Photography courses or certifications are helpful.

🧠 Skills Required

Creativity, Attention to Detail, Camera Handling, Photo Editing, Communication.

🎓 Education

Diploma or Degree in Photography, Mass Communication, or Visual Arts (optional).

💼 Career Opportunities

Wedding Photographer, Wildlife Photographer, Fashion Photographer, Photojournalist, Freelance Photographer.

💰 Average Salary

₹3–12 LPA (can be much higher for experienced professionals).

📈 Future Scope

Photography has excellent opportunities in media, advertising, social media, filmmaking, events, and freelance work.
