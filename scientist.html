🧪 Scientist

About

Scientists conduct research, perform experiments, and develop new discoveries that improve our lives.

📚 Required Subjects

Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Mathematics

🧠 Skills Required

Research, Observation, Critical Thinking, Curiosity

🎓 Education

B.Sc., M.Sc., and often a Ph.D.

💼 Career Opportunities

Research Scientist, Space Scientist, Environmental Scientist, Laboratory Scientist.

💰 Average Salary

₹5–18 LPA

📈 Future Scope

Excellent opportunities in ISRO, DRDO, CSIR, research labs, and universities.
