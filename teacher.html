👩‍🏫 Teacher

About

Teachers educate students, inspire learning, and help build knowledge and skills for the future.

📚 Required Subjects

Any stream based on the subject you want to teach.

🧠 Skills Required

Communication, Patience, Leadership, Creativity, Problem Solving

🎓 Education

Bachelor's Degree, B.Ed., or relevant teaching qualification.

💼 Career Opportunities

School Teacher, College Lecturer, Online Educator, Tutor, Education Consultant.

💰 Average Salary

₹3–10 LPA (depends on institution and experience).

📈 Future Scope

Teachers are always needed in schools, colleges, coaching institutes, and online learning platforms.
