⚖️ Lawyer

About

Lawyers advise clients, represent them in courts, and help people understand and protect their legal rights.

📚 Required Subjects

Any stream in Class 12, followed by an LLB degree.

🧠 Skills Required

Communication, Critical Thinking, Research, Negotiation, Confidence

🎓 Education

BA LLB, BBA LLB, or LLB from a recognized university.

💼 Career Opportunities

Advocate, Corporate Lawyer, Legal Advisor, Judge (after eligibility), Public Prosecutor.

💰 Average Salary

₹4–15 LPA (can increase significantly with experience).

📈 Future Scope

Law offers excellent opportunities in courts, companies, government departments, and private practice.
