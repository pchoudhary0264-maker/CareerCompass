👨‍⚕️ Doctor

About

Doctors diagnose diseases, treat patients, perform medical procedures, and help people live healthier lives.

📚 Required Subjects

Biology, Chemistry, Physics

🧠 Skills Required

Communication, Compassion, Decision Making, Problem Solving, Patience

🎓 Popular Colleges in India

AIIMS, JIPMER, CMC Vellore, AFMC Pune, Government Medical Colleges

💼 Career Opportunities

General Physician, Surgeon, Pediatrician, Cardiologist, Dentist, Medical Researcher

💰 Average Salary

₹6–20 LPA (can increase with specialization and experience)

📈 Future Scope

Healthcare professionals are always in demand. Doctors have excellent career opportunities in hospitals, clinics, research, and public health.
