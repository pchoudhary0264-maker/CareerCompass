✈️ Pilot

About

Pilots fly aircraft safely, transport passengers and cargo, and ensure safe air travel.

📚 Required Subjects

Physics and Mathematics in Class 12.

🧠 Skills Required

Concentration, Decision Making, Leadership, Confidence.

🎓 Education

Commercial Pilot License (CPL) from a recognized flying school.

💼 Career Opportunities

Airline Pilot, Cargo Pilot, Defence Pilot, Flight Instructor.

💰 Average Salary

₹10–40 LPA

📈 Future Scope

Growing demand in commercial aviation and defence services.
